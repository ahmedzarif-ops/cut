Before contacting support

Do not send a password, sign-in code, full date of birth, body weight, calorie or macro history, health details, payment credentials, or an unredacted screenshot. CUT OS support should never ask for those items in an ordinary support request.

The final support form and approved minimum account-verification fields remain to be defined: {{SUPPORT_INTAKE_PROCESS}}.

Subscription help

Restore a purchase

  1. Sign in to the CUT OS account that should receive access.
  2. Open the subscription screen.
  3. Select Restore Purchases.

CUT OS identifies paid access using the signed-in CUT OS account and the active Apple entitlement reported through RevenueCat.

Manage or cancel

Manage an Apple subscription from the in-app Manage Subscription control or visit Apple’s subscription management page. Cancellation does not remove access before the paid entitlement expires.

Delete a CUT OS account

  1. Open Settings in CUT OS.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation and approve the permanent deletion.
  4. Follow the in-app status if deletion needs to resume or retry.

The app is designed to keep deletion available from restricted Settings even when an account is not eligible for the adults-only service. Deletion is complete only after the app reports the terminal result.

Deleting a CUT OS account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel Apple billing separately before or after the deletion.

If the user cannot access the in-app control, the approved assisted deletion route will be: {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}}.

Adults-only access

CUT OS is for people age 18 or older. The app processes a self-declared full date of birth for the current eligibility decision and stores only the result, policy version, and decision time. This is not independent age verification.

Under the current adult-18-v1 design, the first result is permanent for that Clerk identity. An ineligible user can open restricted Settings, sign out, or delete the account, but cannot retry or correct the date in the same account. Later access as an adult would require deletion and creation of a new account.

This handling and the final escalation process remain subject to owner, support, privacy, and legal approval before launch.

Basic troubleshooting

  • Confirm the device is online and try the action again once.
  • Confirm CUT OS is signed in to the intended account.
  • For paid access, use Restore Purchases instead of purchasing repeatedly.
  • Do not create a second account to work around a pending deletion or uncertain purchase state; use the approved support route once it is available.
