## Response acceptance checklist

Before accepting a quote:

- verify the named professional's active qualification and relevant scope;
- require a fixed fee or an explicit not-to-exceed amount;
- require written conclusions, approval, or exact redlines rather than a call
  alone;
- confirm turnaround and included revision rounds;
- reject any scope that leaves a launch-critical question unanswered unless a
  second qualified reviewer is already assigned to it;
- add every accepted amount to the combined budget ledger before payment; and
- preserve reviewer output privately, committing only non-confidential approval
  evidence and approved public text.
