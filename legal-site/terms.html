1. Agreement and eligibility

The intended rule is that a person must be at least 18 years old to create or use a CUT OS account. CUT OS uses a self-declared date of birth to make an adults-only eligibility decision. This is not independent age or identity verification.

The first decision is permanent for that Clerk identity under the current adult-18-v1 policy. An ineligible account can use restricted Settings, sign out, or request deletion, but receives no guidance or purchase path. The owner, support lead, and counsel must approve this rule and its user-facing explanation before launch.

2. Current service

The focused CUT OS launch includes one daily next action, one daily weigh-in, six curated balanced-meal choices, meal logging, editing and deletion, estimated daily nutrition totals, cloud persistence, paid access, and account controls.

Features described elsewhere but not present in the submitted app are not part of the launch service. The final terms must be checked against the final binary and App Store listing.

3. General fitness and nutrition information

CUT OS is a general fitness and nutrition check-in tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical care. Nutrition values are estimates. Ingredient and common-allergen information is not a guarantee that a food is safe for an allergy, medical condition, or individual dietary need. Users must review actual ingredients and product labels.

Qualified nutrition and legal reviewers must approve the final recipes, calculations, labels, claims, and warnings before they are offered to the public.

4. Accounts and acceptable use

The current service requires an authenticated account for private features. A user should protect access to the account and provide accurate information for the actions they choose to complete.

Final account-license, prohibited-use, service-suspension, and termination language is pending counsel review: {{ACCOUNT_AND_ACCEPTABLE_USE_TERMS}}.

5. Auto-renewable subscriptions

  • Paid CUT OS access is intended to use an Apple auto-renewable subscription.
  • The App Store purchase screen shows the actual localized price, billing period, and any approved introductory offer before purchase.
  • The app provides Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription controls.
  • A subscription renews automatically until it is canceled through Apple, subject to the terms shown by Apple at purchase.
  • An active RevenueCat entitlement is the service’s source for paid access; cancellation by itself does not remove access before the paid entitlement expires.

Users can manage or cancel an Apple subscription at Apple’s subscription management page. Final commercial details must match the App Store product approved by the owner; no price or trial is promised in this draft.

6. Account deletion

A signed-in user can request account deletion from Settings. The current workflow is designed to remove the authentication identity, linked CUT OS account data, and the RevenueCat customer used by CUT OS. A minimal deletion-coordination record may remain under an approved retention policy to prevent account recreation and recover partial failures.

Deleting the CUT OS account does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase transaction records held by Apple. Apple subscription cancellation is a separate action.

7. Privacy

The handling of account, age-decision, body, fitness, nutrition, purchase, and deletion information must be described in the final Privacy Policy. The Privacy Policy remains a draft and cannot take effect until the operator and counsel approve it.
