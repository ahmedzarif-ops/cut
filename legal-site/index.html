Current product boundary

CUT OS is an adults-only mobile app for lifters who are cutting. The current launch scope is one daily next action, a daily weigh-in, six curated balanced-meal choices, meal logging, estimated daily nutrition totals, cloud persistence, subscriptions, and account controls. It is a general fitness and nutrition tool, not medical care.
