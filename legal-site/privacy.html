1. Scope

This draft covers information processed when an adult uses the CUT OS mobile app, its account services, paid subscription features, and support. CUT OS is intended only for people age 18 or older.

The repository’s working launch position is the United States. Final availability by state or country has not been approved.

3. Date-of-birth processing

CUT OS sends the full date of birth to its server to make a terminal self-declared age-eligibility decision under policy adult-18-v1. A request may be retried if the app has not confirmed a terminal result. The raw date is used in memory for each request and is designed not to be stored, logged, cached, returned in the response, added to Clerk, or sent to analytics or crash reporting. Only the eligibility result, policy version, and decision time are stored with the account.

An “eligible” result means the date entered met the rule. It is not identity verification or independent proof of age.

4. How information is used

  • Authenticate the user and keep account data separated.
  • Enforce deletion status and adults-only access.
  • Provide weigh-in, meal, nutrition-total, and next-action features.
  • Verify paid access and restore or manage purchases.
  • Protect action integrity and recover interrupted writes or deletion.
  • Deliver requests, limit abuse, and protect authentication.
  • Respond to account and product support requests.

The project rule prohibits placing body weight, calories, macros, measurements, or raw date of birth in URLs, routine logs, analytics events, or third-party customer attributes. Final production configuration must be tested against that rule before launch.

5. Service providers and platforms

CUT OS currently depends on the following services:

  • Clerk for authentication and account identity.
  • Apple for App Store distribution and subscription transactions.
  • RevenueCat for subscription entitlement and purchase status. CUT OS is designed to send RevenueCat only the internal CUT OS user UUID, not the Clerk ID, email, name, date of birth, weight, measurements, meal data, or nutrition values.

The current application dependency inventory contains no advertising SDK or general product-analytics SDK. RevenueCat supplies purchase and subscription analytics. This statement must be rechecked against the final production archive and live vendor configuration.

6. Tracking and advertising

The current app configuration has tracking disabled and declares no tracking domains. CUT OS does not use body, fitness, meal, nutrition, purchase, or eligibility data for advertising audiences or tracking. Final App Store privacy answers must match the shipped app and every embedded service.

7. Storage, security, and retention

Account, profile, weight, meal, eligibility-result, and deletion-state records are held in CUT OS systems or the providers described above. Session and recovery records are held in secure device storage where implemented. CUT OS uses server-side account boundaries and avoids routine logging of request bodies and sensitive fitness values.

Retention is unresolved: {{RETENTION_SCHEDULE}}. Before publication, the operator must approve specific rules for active-account data, database backups, hosting logs, Clerk records and events, RevenueCat records, support messages, deletion tombstones, and build or crash diagnostics. The linked meal deletion safeguard is designed to delete with the CUT OS user; backup handling remains part of this open schedule.

No security method can guarantee absolute protection. Production security controls and incident contact language still require review.

8. Account deletion

A signed-in user can request account deletion from Settings in the app. The implemented workflow is designed to delete the Clerk identity, delete the CUT OS user record and its linked profile, weight, and meal records, and delete the RevenueCat customer associated with the internal CUT OS UUID. A minimal deletion-coordination record may remain to prevent account recreation and recover partial failures; its final retention period is not approved.

Deleting a CUT OS account does not cancel an Apple subscription and does not erase transaction records held by Apple. Subscription management is a separate Apple action at Apple’s subscription management page.

A verified public route for people who cannot access the in-app deletion control is still required: {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}}.

9. Access, correction, and privacy choices

Users can edit supported profile, weight, and meal information in the app and can delete their account in Settings. Purchase management and cancellation occur through Apple.

The final policy must describe the verified request process, identity checks, response rules, exceptions, appeals or complaints, and any region-specific rights after launch scope and applicable law are reviewed by counsel. Contact route: {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}}.

10. Adults-only service

CUT OS is intended only for people age 18 or older. An account marked ineligible receives no fitness guidance or purchase path, but can reach restricted Settings, sign out, or request account deletion. The self-declared age flow, notice timing, underage-attempt handling, and permanent decision for each identity require final privacy and legal approval before launch.
