App Store requirements are satisfied, with lawyer and dietitian discussions to

repository's earlier internal rule that qualified legal and nutrition review
must precede App Store submission. It does not state or imply that a lawyer,
dietitian, clinician, regulator, Apple, or any other professional has approved

No email, message, engagement, payment, or other external communication is
authorized by this record. Separate owner confirmation is still required before
contacting any external person.

For an objective deadline, "the first few days" is implemented as follows:

- initiate qualified technology/privacy legal review within three calendar days

- initiate qualified nutrition review of the exact shipped catalog, calculations,
  allergen language, and product copy within the same three-calendar-day window;

- do not mark either review approved until a named qualified reviewer supplies a

   nutritionally-complete, allergen-safe, or professional-approval claims;
3. label nutrition totals as estimates, identify ingredient and allergen limits,

4. pass exact signed-build authentication, age-gate, purchase, restore, account

6. preserve all reviewer gaps as open post-launch work rather than changing them

## Stop-sales and remediation policy

The owner accepts these launch controls:

- If either review is not initiated within three calendar days after public
  release, stop new subscription sales and remove the app from availability
  until the review is initiated or the owner records a new explicit decision.
- If a reviewer, regulator, Apple, or credible security/privacy report identifies
  a critical legal, safety, privacy, nutrition, allergen, or misleading-claims
  issue, stop new sales and public availability while the issue is corrected.
- Implement material reviewer corrections promptly, update public disclosures
  and App Store answers when required, and submit a corrected binary if shipped
  behavior or claims change.
- Do not silently treat post-launch review as optional or completed.
