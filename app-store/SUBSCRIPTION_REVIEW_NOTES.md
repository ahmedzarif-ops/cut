# CUT OS Pro Monthly — credential-free review notes

**Status:** Working copy for the next exact release build. Not yet saved in App
Store Connect.

CUT OS is free to download and its core tracking loop does not require a
subscription. Free users can search the reviewed food catalog, browse all
current balanced meal templates, use supported barcode lookup, add custom and
saved foods, log food, record weigh-ins and training, and review progress.

CUT OS Pro Monthly adds personalized adaptive meal fits and on-demand meal
drafts based on the user's goals, nutrition targets, explicit preferences,
liked or hidden meals, confirmed meal history, and the current day's remaining
calories and protein. Generated drafts are constrained to active catalog foods
and gram amounts. The CUT OS server calculates nutrition values and never
auto-logs a generated result.

Product ID: `com.zarifahmed.cut.pro.monthly`

Duration: one month

Launch storefront and price: United States, $4.99 per month

Introductory offer: none

Family Sharing: off

To review the subscription in the exact build, sign in with the review account,
complete the adults-only eligibility step, open Food, and choose a Pro meal-fit
or meal-draft action. The app then presents the StoreKit-localized CUT OS Pro
Monthly offer before purchase. Restore Purchases is available from the offer
screen and Settings. Manage App Store Subscription opens Apple's subscription
management page. Paid access is granted only after the CUT server confirms the
`CUT_OS_PRO` entitlement.

Food and meal nutrition is estimated. CUT OS provides general fitness and
nutrition information, not medical advice. Review credentials belong only in
App Store Connect or the approved secret manager, never in this file.
