# CUT OS — preliminary name screen

**Checked:** August 3, 2026
**Status:** Preliminary evidence only; not legal clearance, registration, or an
App Store name reservation.

This record preserves the narrow, read-only searches completed before the CUT
OS 1.0 App Store record exists. It must not be used to claim that the name is
available or non-infringing.

## Direct results

### United States federal trademark database

The official USPTO Trademark Search interface was queried using its documented
field syntax:

- Exact full-mark query `FM:"CUT OS"`: **0 results**.
- Normalized combined-mark query `CM:CUTOS`: **1 result**, registration
  `2916346`, serial `78329403`, for bendable beaded toys in class 028. The
  official record shows **Dead / Cancelled**, with cancellation dated September
  3, 2014.

References:

### United States App Store search surface

Apple's public United States software-search response for the term `CUT OS`
returned 143 relevance results at the time of the check. Within that response:

- no app title exactly matched `CUT OS`; and
- no returned title contained the normalized string `CUTOS` after ignoring

Reference:

### Similar same-category title found outside the exact-name result

The exact-name result above does not remove market or naming risk. A separate
same-category review found Apple's United States listing for **Body Recomp
Tracker - Cut**, subtitle **Fat Loss + Muscle, Tracked**, in Health & Fitness.
Its listing describes weight trends, protein and macro logging, and strength
tracking for people cutting or recomping, and shows an in-app purchase named
**Cut Pro** at **$4.99** at the time of this check.

Reference:

- [Body Recomp Tracker - Cut on the U.S. App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/body-recomp-tracker-cut/id6772375243)

This is factual market context, not a legal conclusion about similarity,
priority, availability, or infringement. It increases the importance of the
existing counsel-clearance and owner naming gates before an app record is
created.

## What this does not prove

This screen does not cover every confusingly similar spelling, sound, design,
translation, related good or service, state registration, common-law use,
foreign territory, domain, company name, or unindexed product. It is not a
likelihood-of-confusion opinion. Apple's public search is relevance-based and
does not reserve a name or prove that App Store Connect will accept it.

## Release gate

Before the public listing is submitted:

1. qualified trademark counsel must complete and document a comprehensive
   clearance appropriate to the approved launch territories and goods/services;
2. the owner must approve the final product name; and
3. the live App Store Connect app record must accept the exact name.

Until all three are complete, public name availability remains unresolved.
