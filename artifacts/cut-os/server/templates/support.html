Before contacting support

Do not send a password, sign-in code, full date of birth, body weight, calorie or macro history, health details, payment credentials, or an unredacted screenshot. Ordinary CUT OS support should not ask for those items.

The approved minimum account-verification fields and intake process are pending: {{SUPPORT_INTAKE_PROCESS}}.

Subscription help

Restore a purchase

  1. Sign in to the intended CUT OS account.
  2. Open the subscription screen.
  3. Select Restore Purchases.

CUT OS resolves paid access from the signed-in CUT OS account and the active Apple entitlement reported through RevenueCat.

Manage or cancel

Use the in-app Manage Subscription control or visit Apple’s subscription management page. Cancellation does not remove access before the paid entitlement expires.

Delete a CUT OS account

  1. Open Settings in CUT OS.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Read and approve the permanent-deletion confirmation.
  4. Follow the in-app status if deletion needs to resume or retry.

Restricted Settings keep deletion available when an account is not eligible for the adults-only service. Deleting CUT OS does not cancel Apple billing; manage or cancel that subscription separately.

Assisted deletion for someone who cannot access the app is pending: {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}}.

Adults-only access

CUT OS is for people age 18 or older. The app sends a self-declared date of birth for one eligibility decision and stores only the result, policy version, and decision time. This is not independent age verification.

Under adult-18-v1, the first result is permanent for that Clerk identity. An ineligible user can use restricted Settings, sign out, or delete the account, but cannot retry the date in that account. Later adult access would require deleting it and creating a new account. This support path still requires owner, privacy, and legal approval.

Basic troubleshooting

  • Confirm the device is online and retry the action once.
  • Confirm CUT OS is signed in to the intended account.
  • For paid access, use Restore Purchases rather than purchasing repeatedly.
  • Do not create another account to work around pending deletion or an uncertain purchase state; use the approved support route once active.
