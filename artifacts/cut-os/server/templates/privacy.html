Scope and audience

This draft covers information processed through the CUT OS mobile app, account service, paid features, and support. CUT OS is intended only for people age 18 or older. The repository records a United States working launch position; final availability has not been approved.

Date-of-birth processing

CUT OS sends a self-declared full date of birth to its server to make a terminal decision under adult-18-v1. A request may be retried if the app has not confirmed a terminal result. The raw date is designed to exist only in memory for each request and not be persisted, logged, cached, returned, added to Clerk, or sent to analytics or crash reporting. The account stores only the result, policy version, and decision time. An eligible result is not identity or age verification.

How information is used

  • Authenticate users and separate account data.
  • Enforce account-deletion status and adults-only access.
  • Provide weigh-in, meal, daily-total, and next-action features.
  • Verify paid access and restore or manage purchases.
  • Protect action integrity and recover interrupted actions.
  • Deliver requests, limit abuse, and protect authentication.
  • Respond to account and product support requests.

The current product rule prohibits body weight, calories, macros, measurements, or raw date of birth in URLs, routine logs, analytics events, or third-party customer attributes. Final production behavior must be tested against that rule.

Platforms and providers

  • Clerk provides authentication and account identity.
  • Apple provides App Store distribution and subscription transactions.
  • RevenueCat provides subscription entitlement and purchase status. CUT OS is designed to identify the customer with only an internal CUT OS UUID, not a Clerk ID, email, name, date of birth, weight, measurements, meal data, or nutrition values.

The current dependency inventory contains no advertising SDK or general product-analytics SDK. RevenueCat supplies purchase and subscription analytics. The final production archive and live vendor configuration remain the evidence.

Tracking and advertising

The current app configuration has tracking disabled and no tracking domains. CUT OS does not use body, fitness, meal, nutrition, purchase, or eligibility data for advertising audiences or tracking. Final App Store privacy answers must match the shipped app and its providers.

Security and retention

The current design uses server-side account boundaries, secure device storage for session and recovery state, and avoids routine logging of request bodies and sensitive fitness values. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Retention is unresolved: {{RETENTION_SCHEDULE}}. The final schedule must cover active-account data, database backups, hosting logs, Clerk and RevenueCat records, support messages, deletion tombstones, and build or crash diagnostics. The linked meal deletion safeguard is designed to delete with the CUT OS user; backup handling remains part of this open schedule.

Account deletion

A signed-in user can request deletion in Settings. The current workflow is designed to delete the Clerk identity, CUT OS account and linked profile, weight, and meal records, and the RevenueCat customer linked to the internal CUT OS UUID. A minimal coordination record may remain to prevent account recreation and finish interrupted deletion; its retention period is not approved.

Deleting a CUT OS account does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase transaction records held by Apple. Subscription management is a separate Apple action at Apple’s subscription management page.

Choices and contact

Users can edit supported profile, weight, and meal information in the app and can delete the account in Settings. Apple manages purchase cancellation.
