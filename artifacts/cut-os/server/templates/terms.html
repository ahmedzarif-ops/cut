Eligibility

The intended rule is that a person must be at least 18 years old to create or use a CUT OS account. CUT OS uses a self-declared date of birth under adult-18-v1; that decision is not independent identity or age verification.

Under the current design, the first result is permanent for that Clerk identity. An ineligible account can use restricted Settings, sign out, or request deletion, but receives no guidance or purchase path. Owner, support, privacy, and legal approval remain required.

Current service

The focused launch includes one daily next action, one daily weigh-in, six curated balanced-meal choices, meal logging, editing and deletion, estimated daily nutrition totals, cloud persistence, paid access, and account controls. The final terms must match the submitted binary and App Store listing.

General fitness and nutrition information

CUT OS is a general fitness and nutrition coaching tool, not medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical care. Nutrition values are estimates. Ingredient and common-allergen information does not guarantee that a food is safe for an allergy, medical condition, or individual dietary need. Users must review actual ingredients and product labels.

Qualified nutrition and legal reviewers must approve recipes, calculations, labels, claims, and warnings before public launch.

Accounts

The current service requires an authenticated account for private features. A user should protect account access and provide accurate information for the actions they choose to complete.

Account license, acceptable-use, suspension, and termination language remains pending: {{ACCOUNT_AND_ACCEPTABLE_USE_TERMS}}.

Auto-renewable subscriptions

  • Paid access is intended to use an Apple auto-renewable subscription.
  • Apple’s purchase screen shows the localized price, billing period, and any approved introductory offer before purchase.
  • The app provides restore and subscription-management controls.
  • An active RevenueCat entitlement controls paid access. Cancellation does not remove access before that entitlement expires.

Manage or cancel with Apple. The final commercial details must match the product the owner approves; no price or trial is promised here.

Account deletion

A signed-in user can request deletion in Settings. The current workflow is designed to remove the Clerk identity, linked CUT OS data, and the RevenueCat customer. A minimal coordination record may remain under an approved retention policy to prevent recreation and recover failures.

Deleting CUT OS does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple’s transaction records. Apple cancellation is a separate action.
